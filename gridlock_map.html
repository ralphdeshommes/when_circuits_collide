  • Two dots joined by a line are one project: the dots are its substations. The line is drawn straight, not along the real route.
  • A project with only one location found has no line — just a larger shape. A square is Dominion, a circle is Georgia Power. Hollow means it overlaps nothing.
  • Pick a card and the map draws a 25-mile circle around that Dominion project's centre, marked D.
  • The numbered badges are the Georgia Power projects inside that circle. The detail card lists which number is which.
  • You can also click any project, either company. It becomes the new centre, and the other company's projects inside its circle are numbered.
  • Red glow means the project takes part in an overlap.
  • Dotted means a low-confidence location.
